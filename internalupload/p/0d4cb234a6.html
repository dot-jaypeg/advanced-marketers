Questions We Get Every Summer

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, call (702) 682-8439 and ask.

How much does it cost to get my air conditioner repaired?

We price transparently and upfront — no hidden fees, no surprises. A technician diagnoses the issue and gives you a flat-rate quote before starting any repair, so you know exactly what to expect.

Considering a replacement instead? There's currently $1,500 off full HVAC system replacements, and an energy-efficient system lowers both your power bills and your future repair costs. Call (702) 682-8439 for a repair quote or a free consultation on which route makes sense.

Should I repair or replace my air conditioner?

A few signs it's time to upgrade:

  • The unit is 10–15 years old
  • Repairs cost more than half the price of a new system
  • Energy bills keep climbing despite repairs
  • You're calling for service over and over

We install energy-efficient systems and offer financing to make the upgrade affordable. Call for a free consultation and we'll help you make the call.

How much does it cost to replace an AC unit in Las Vegas?

It depends on the size and brand of the unit, its efficiency rating, and how complex the installation is. During a free consultation, our technicians assess your specific setup and give you a transparent, upfront quote before any work begins.

How long does an air conditioner last in Las Vegas?

More than most homeowners expect. Typical lifespans here:

  • 15–20 years for a well-maintained unit
  • 20–25+ years with regular professional maintenance
  • 10–15 years with minimal upkeep or a rough location

Twice-yearly professional service can add five or more years. Filter changes matter, especially with pets. Units sitting in full direct sun tend to run shorter lives. Start budgeting for replacement once your system passes 15 years, and if you can plan it, winter is the cheaper season to install.

Is it worth repairing a 20-year-old AC unit?

Usually replacement wins on long-term value. At 20+ years the unit has already outlived its expected 15–20 year life, R22 refrigerant in older systems has been phased out (making repairs pricier every year), and new systems run roughly 30–40% more efficiently.

Repairs may be cheaper today, but frequent ones add up fast. Our licensed technicians will look at your unit's actual condition, your budget and how long you plan to stay in the home, then give you an honest recommendation. Call (702) 682-8439 for a free assessment.
