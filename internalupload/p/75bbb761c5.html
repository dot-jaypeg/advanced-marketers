What this means for ad buying: at 15% churn a $2,500/mo client is worth ~$16,667. Meta acquired 1 direct client + 2 diagnosis buyers for $4,370 → 4.04x LTV-ROAS. ChatGPT acquired Scott Stevens for $5,068 → 3.29x LTV-ROAS. Both channels look unprofitable on first-month cash but strongly profitable on LTV. Break-even CAC is ~$25k/client; you're paying $4–5k — room to scale as long as churn holds at or below 15%.
Assumptions to validate: (1) won-deal value = monthly retainer (one-time projects lower LTV); (2) actual churn — at 10% LTV rises to $38,125, at 20% it falls to $19,063.
