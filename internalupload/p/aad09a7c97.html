PRO INEGRITY

The name isn't a slogan. It's the standard we work to.

Plumbing goes wrong in the walls, under the slab, and behind the drywall — places you can't check for yourself. So the only thing that really protects you is the character of the person holding the wrench.

Honesty

You get the actual diagnosis in plain language, plus the parts and labor written down before anything starts. If a repair will hold, we say so instead of selling you a replacement.

Integrity

Quality of work

What we fix

Repairs, replacements,
and remodels.

Residential plumbing across Apple Valley and the surrounding High Desert. If it carries water, drains water, or is supposed to and isn't, it's our work.

Bathroom & Kitchen Remodels

Rough-in through trim-out: moving supply and drain lines, setting tubs, showers, vanities and sinks so the finish work lands clean and the fixtures sit where you want them.

Camera and Location

Camera inspection and line location. We send a camera down the line, watch the blockage or break on screen, and mark exactly where it sits — before anyone cuts concrete.

Drain Cleaning

Kitchen, bath, laundry and main lines cleared with the right cable and cutter head for the pipe — then we look at why it clogged, so you're not calling again next month.

Electronic Leak Detection

Acoustic and pressure equipment to pinpoint a hidden leak under slab, in a wall, or in the yard — so the repair is one opening instead of five exploratory holes.

Faucet Repair and Replace

Cartridges, stems and seats rebuilt where the fixture is worth saving — or a clean swap to a new faucet with new supply lines and a proper seal at the deck.

Pipe Leak Repair

Copper, PEX, CPVC and galvanized: cut out the failed section, repair it correctly, and pressure test before it goes back behind the wall. Repipes when a patch won't hold.

Sewer Repair

Root intrusion, bellied lines, cracked clay and collapsed cast iron. We locate the failure, dig only where we have to, and replace it with new pipe at the right fall.

Shower Valve Repair and Replace

Leaking or seized mixing valves rebuilt or replaced, with attention to the tile and surround so the fix doesn't turn into a remodel you didn't ask for.
