1. The Universal Call Flow

Every call, every trade, same skeleton. Only the discovery questions change — pull those from the trade module. Mindset: you are not the show — they are. Don't pitch. Ask smart questions, stay curious, validate everything on the lead form, and avoid jargon.

Step 1 — Opening (inbound)

"Thanks for calling [Client Company], this is [Name] — how can I help you today?"

(Let them talk. Do NOT interrupt — collect the problem, urgency, and their exact words.)

"Got it — sorry you're dealing with that. Let me ask you a couple quick questions so we can get the right person out to you, is that okay?" (Get a clear yes.)

Step 1B — Opening (outbound to a lead that didn't answer)

"Hi, is this [First Name]? This is [Name] with [Client Company] — you reached out to us on Facebook about [service]. Is it okay if I ask you a couple quick questions so we can get this handled for you?"

(Never ask "is now a good time?" — ask permission to ask questions instead. On a yes, go straight into discovery.)

(If "how did you get my number?") "You filled out a quick form on our ad — you were asking about [service]. Is that still something you need handled?"

Step 2 — Universal discovery (every trade)

  • Problem: "Walk me through what's going on — what are you seeing?"
  • Urgency: "How long has this been going on? Is it getting worse?"
  • Property: "House or business? Do you own the property?"
  • Location: "What area are you in?" (confirm service area BEFORE booking)
  • History: "Has anyone else looked at it? What did they say?" (surfaces price shopping)
  • Decision: "If we can take care of it, are you good to move forward, or does anyone else weigh in?"

Then add 3–4 questions from the trade module below. While they talk: log job type, urgency, estimated value (client's average ticket), and their exact words.

Step 3 — Recap + book (the goal close)

"Just to recap — you've got [their problem, their words], it's been going on [time], and you want it handled [urgency]. Right?" (Wait for the yes.)

"Perfect. We can have [a tech / an estimator / a crew] out [day/time] or [alternate day/time] — which works better for you?"

(Always alternate-choice. Always book while on the phone.)

"You're all set for [day/time]. You'll get a text confirmation right now and a reminder the day before. [Tech] will call on the way. Anything else I can help with?"

Step 4 — Wrap

  • Notes, opportunity value, stage move, confirmation text — 2 minutes max.
  • Notify the tech/company of the booking and get dispatch confirmation.
Trade Module

Plumbing

Urgency-driven. Water damage fear is real — use it honestly. Emergencies book same-day.

Trade discovery

  • "Is water actively leaking right now? Have you been able to shut it off?" (Yes to active leak = emergency, book same-day, escalate to owner if no slot)
  • "Where is it — kitchen, bathroom, water heater, main line, outside?"
  • "How old is the home / the water heater?" (10+ yr water heater = likely replacement, bigger ticket)
  • "Any other slow drains or issues around the house while we're at it?" (bundles work, raises ticket)

Urgency triggers → same-day booking

Active leak, no hot water, sewage backup, burst pipe, no water at all. Script: "That's the kind of thing that gets worse and more expensive by the hour — let me get someone out today. Morning or afternoon?"

Trade-specific objection

"Can't you just tell me the price over the phone?" → "For [drain clearing / standard jobs] I can give you the starting range: [$X]. For anything beyond that, honest answer — anyone quoting a leak sight-unseen is guessing. The visit is [free / $X per client policy], and you get a real number. [Day] or [day]?"

Trade Module

Roofing

Big ticket, longer cycle. Two lead types: LEAK (urgent) and REPLACEMENT/INSPECTION (considered). Insurance changes everything — flag it.

Trade discovery

  • "Are you seeing a leak inside, or is this more about the age/condition of the roof?" (splits urgent vs. considered)
  • "How old is the roof, and do you know what it's made of — shingle, tile, flat?"
  • "Was there any recent storm or wind damage?" (YES → possible insurance claim — note it, big deal for the client)
  • "Have you filed or thought about filing an insurance claim?"
  • "Single story or two story? Roughly how big is the home?"

Booking language

Sell the inspection, not the roof: "Let's get you a free roof inspection — [inspector] gets on the roof, takes photos, and shows you exactly what's going on. If it's storm damage, insurance may cover more than you'd think, and we'll document everything they need. [Day] or [day]?"

Trade-specific objection

"I'm getting three bids." → "Smart — a roof's a big investment. Most folks tell us our inspection report is the most detailed of the three, photos included. Get us out there and use our report to compare the others. When's good?"

Trade Module

HVAC

Comfort emergency in summer/winter, considered purchase in between. System age is the money question.

Trade discovery

  • "Is the system not working at all, or working but not keeping up?" (dead system in a heat wave = emergency, book same-day)
  • "How old is the unit?" (12–15+ years = likely replacement conversation, much bigger ticket — note it)
  • "When was it last serviced?"
  • "Anyone in the home especially affected — kids, elderly, pets?" (real urgency, prioritize)
  • "Have your energy bills been creeping up?" (efficiency angle for replacements)

Booking language

Repair lead: "Let's get a tech out to diagnose it — [free / $X diagnostic, credited toward the repair]. Today at [time] or tomorrow at [time]?" Aging system: "At that age it's worth having [Company] look at whether a repair or an upgrade makes more sense — they'll give you both numbers, and there are financing options. When works?"

Trade-specific objection

"A new system is too expensive." → "Totally understand — nobody budgets for it. That's why [Client Company] offers financing that usually lands around [$X/month], and a new unit cuts the energy bill too. Worth seeing the actual numbers before deciding? The visit costs you nothing."

Trade Module

Flooring

Project-based, zero emergency. The enemy is procrastination — your job is to make the in-home measure feel easy and free of commitment.

Trade discovery

  • "Which rooms are you thinking about, and roughly how much space?" (rooms × ~sq ft = ticket size)
  • "What are you leaning toward — LVP, hardwood, tile, carpet? Or still deciding?"
  • "What's on the floor now, and are you replacing it because of damage, wear, or just time for a change?"
  • "Is there a date you're working toward — holidays, guests, selling the home?" (deadline = real urgency, use it)
  • "Have you gotten samples or quotes anywhere yet?"

Booking language

Sell the measure + samples: "Easiest next step is a free in-home measure — [estimator] brings samples so you see them in your own light, measures everything, and you get an exact price, not a guess. No obligation. [Day] or [day]?"

Trade-specific objection

"We're not ready yet / just pricing." → "Perfect time, actually — the measure is free and the quote is good for [X days]. You'll know your real number, so when you ARE ready there's no scramble. Want me to set it up while it costs nothing?"

Trade Module

Junk Removal

Fast, transactional, price-first callers. Speed IS the product — book the soonest slot, keep calls short (3–5 min).

Trade discovery

  • "What are we hauling — furniture, appliances, yard waste, a cleanout?"
  • "Roughly how much? Like a couch and a few boxes, half a garage, a whole house?" (volume = price — get them to compare to something visual)
  • "Is everything in one spot, or spread out? Any stairs?"
  • "Anything heavy or unusual — pianos, hot tubs, concrete?"
  • "How soon do you want it gone?" (usually the answer is NOW — book the soonest window)

Booking language

"Based on what you described, you're likely in the [$X–$Y] range — [Company] confirms the exact price on-site before touching anything, and if you're not good with the number, they leave, no charge. We've got a crew in your area [today/tomorrow] — [window] or [window]?"

Photo option: "Text me a photo of the pile and I'll get you a tighter estimate in a few minutes." (Great for hesitant callers — and gets their number active in SMS.)

Trade-specific objection

"Just give me a price." → "It's priced by how much space it takes in the truck — that pile you described is probably [$X–$Y]. Crew confirms on-site before starting, walk away free if it's not right. Want the [window] slot before it's gone?"

7. Universal Objection Handling

Framework for every objection, any trade: Probe → Acknowledge → Refocus → Close. Never argue. Never talk past the sale.

ObjectionHandle
"How much is it?"Probe: "Fair question — depends on [factors]. That's why the [estimate / inspection / measure / diagnostic] exists: [free / $X — client policy], and you get a real number instead of a phone guess. [Day] or [day]?"
"That's too expensive""Help me understand — when you say too expensive, what are you comparing it to?" Acknowledge. Refocus on value: licensed, warrantied, done-right-once. "Is that difference worth risking a redo?"
"I need to talk to my spouse""Of course. What questions do you think they'll have? … Let's pencil [day] — if it doesn't work after you talk, one text and we move it."
"I already called someone else""Smart to compare. The [visit] is [free / $X credited], so a second opinion costs you nothing — and folks are usually glad they got one. Want me to set it up?"
"Just looking / not ready"Probe for the real situation, acknowledge, offer the no-pressure next step (free visit / photo estimate / quote that locks pricing), ask for agreement.
"How did you get my number?""You filled out a quick form on our [Facebook/Google] ad about [service]. Is that still something you need handled?"
Never quote "free" by default. Estimate/inspection/diagnostic pricing varies by client — check the client profile first. If it's paid, lead with value: "$X, credited toward the job if you move forward."
