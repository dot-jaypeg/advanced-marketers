Build your client folders

108 folders to create, split by Growth Manager. Find your name, build your list. About 45 seconds each.

The 6 clicks — repeat per client

  1. In ClickUp, click the space name 🚀 AM Delivery 2.0 at the top, then the … that appears next to it.
  2. Choose Templates → Apply a template.
  3. Type NEW CLIENT in the search box and wait for results.
  4. Click NEW CLIENT - Full Setup, then the purple Use Template button.
  5. Clear the Folder name box and type the client's name exactly as written in your list below. Change nothing else.
  6. Click Use Template at the bottom right. Wait for “Template applied”, then repeat.
What you get, already done for you: the folder, the Delivery list, all Overview cards, 14 parent tasks with their subtasks, and Notify When Done already filled in on every task. You do not set those.
⚠ Delete what the client didn't buy — do this every time.
The template is one-size-fits-all. It creates tasks for Google Ads, Meta Ads, Website/CRO, SEO, Yelp, GHL and Video on every single folder, whether that client bought those services or not. On accounts we've been running for months it will also create build tasks for work that was finished long ago.

As soon as the folder is created, open the Delivery list and delete every task that doesn't apply to that client. Services they never bought, and work that's already done. Do not leave them sitting there — they'll show up as overdue, they'll fire notifications at Tim, Kyle, Scotty and Mizael, and the Overview will show a progress bar that means nothing.
Then two more things:
1. Every task lands assigned to you. Reassign to the real owners.
2. The list inside comes over named Delivery — [Client Name]. Rename it to Delivery — <Client>.
3. Set Assignee and Due date on everything that's left.
Only use “NEW CLIENT - Full Setup”. Older templates (AM-New Client Template, KM-NEW CLIENT 2.0) also show up in that search. They do not have the notify routes. Do not use them.

New sales going forward — use the PACKAGE template

From now on, a brand-new client gets built from the template that matches what they bought — same 6 clicks, just pick a different template in step 4. These come with the right tasks already: nothing to delete.
