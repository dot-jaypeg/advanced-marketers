Finding old tasks

Two different things get called "I can't find my old tasks," and they have completely different answers. Figure out which one you're hitting before you go looking.

Situation 1 — fixable in 10 seconds

Recent client work that looks like it vanished

Anything from roughly the last few months. It's in the client's folder in AM Delivery 2.0 right now — two view settings are hiding it.

Skip to “The two switches” below.

Situation 2 — not coming back

Tasks from the old MARKETING PACKAGE space

The pre-2.0 structure. That space was archived on purpose when we rebuilt, and it is staying archived.

Read “About the old space” below.

The two switches

Here's Lincoln Plumbing & Rooter as it looks today. Two tasks:

There are 17 in there. ClickUp hides completed tasks by default, and it collapses subtasks by default. Almost all older work is marked Complete, so the folder reads as empty even when it isn't.

1

Click the gear at the top right of the list

Just left of the purple + Task button. That opens Customize view.

2

Turn on “Show closed tasks,” then set Subtasks → Expanded

3

Everything comes back

Next time it's one click. Once it's on, a green check chip sits in the toolbar — that's the on/off switch, no gear needed.

Don't hit “Save view.” That orange button changes the list for everyone. Leaving it unsaved is fine — it stays on while you're working.

One place instead of folder by folder

Use All Tasks at the top of your sidebar under Spaces. Filter Assignee → you, turn on Show closed tasks, and you get everything you've ever been assigned across every client in one list. Favorite it and it's one click from then on.

About the old space

The old MARKETING PACKAGE space still exists and is fully intact — it's archived, along with SALES & ACQUISITIONS, ONBOARDING, WEB PACKAGE, CREATIVE PACKAGE, QA and Operations.

Archived is deliberate, and it's staying that way. The whole point of 2.0 was one structure, one place per client. Bringing the old spaces back would put us right back to work happening in two systems, which is the problem we just spent weeks fixing.

So if what you're after is genuinely from the old structure: it isn't searchable, and that's on purpose, not a bug you should keep hunting. Your Gmail thread is a legitimate way to find it. If you need something out of there that you can't reconstruct, ask Chris rather than working around it — one-off pulls are fine, reopening the old world isn't.

Worth knowing: a lot of what feels like "old space" work is actually Situation 1. Before assuming something is gone, turn on closed tasks in the client folder — that covers most of it.
