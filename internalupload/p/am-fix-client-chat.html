Advanced Marketers · ClientUp 2.0 fix

Fix your Client Chat

Right now almost every client folder's Client Chat is pointing at the same place — the CLIENT TEMPLATE channel. So when you type a message in a client's folder, it isn't going to that client's channel. It's going into one shared template channel that everyone else is also writing into.

That's why the chat looks identical no matter which account you open. Takes about two minutes per account to fix.

This is not your fault and you didn't break it. Every client folder was copied from a template, and the copy carried the template's chat with it. The setup step that fixes it got closed by mistake during a cleanup, so it never got done on any account.

First — which case are you in?

Open your client's folder and look at the row of tabs at the top.

Case 1 — says "Add Channel"

No channel exists

You need to create one first, then point the card at it.

Do Part A, then Part B, then Part C.

Case 2 — says "Channel"

Channel already exists

You just need to point the card at it.

Skip Part A. Do Part B and Part C.

Part A — Create the channel

Only if your tab bar says "Add Channel".

1

Click "Add Channel" in the tab bar

It's the greyed-out item on the far left, next to Overview.

2

Name it after the client

Use the client's real name exactly as the folder is named — Axxels Plumbing Corp, not "chat" or "client" or an abbreviation. The name is how everyone else finds it later.

3

Add the people who work this account

At minimum: the Growth Specialist, the Growth Manager, and whoever is doing the build. If you're not sure, add fewer — people can be added later, but a message sent to the wrong audience can't be unsent.

Do not add the client themselves unless Chris or your manager tells you to. This is our internal channel for the account.

Part B — Copy the channel link

1

Click the "Channel" tab

Same tab bar, far left. It should now say Channel, not "Add Channel".

2

Copy the whole URL out of your browser's address bar

It looks like this:

https://app.clickup.com/10552018/v/cn/a20pj-158834

The part at the end is the bit that matters — it's unique to this client's channel.

Copy it now, before you click anything else. The moment you navigate away you lose it and have to come back.

Part C — Point the Client Chat card at it

1

Go to the Overview tab and scroll to Client Chat

You'll see it's showing CLIENT TEMPLATE — Speedee Drains. That's the bug.

2

Hover the card, click the gear icon

Three small icons appear at the top-right of the card when you hover it. The gear is the middle one.

3

Replace the Embed URL

Select the whole field and delete it first. Then paste your client's URL.

before → https://app.clickup.com/10552018/v/cn/a20pj-147534 after → https://app.clickup.com/10552018/v/cn/a20pj-158834

Don't just click and type. If you don't clear the field first you end up with both URLs stuck together and the card breaks. Click into the field, select all (⌘A on Mac, Ctrl+A on Windows), delete, then paste.

4

Close the panel and check it worked

Click the ✕ at the top right. The card should now show your client's name, and the message box at the bottom should say "Write to [your client]".

That's the test. If the box still says "Write to CLIENT TEMPLATE — Speedee Drains", it didn't save — go back and check the URL doesn't have the old one stuck on the end.

Worth knowing

  • Check the message box, not the header. The header can look right while the embed is still wrong. The "Write to..." text is the reliable one.
  • One folder at a time, finish each one. Half-done is worse than not started, because it looks fixed.
  • If the old template channel has real messages about your client in it — anything about payments, launches, client requests — copy them into the new channel before you move on. There's a week of conversation sitting in there.
  • Tell your manager when your accounts are done so nobody redoes them.
Anything that doesn't match what's on your screen — stop and ask rather than guessing. Getting one wrong is easy to fix; getting twenty wrong quietly is not.
