Onboarding a New Client

From signed contract to a working client folder. Atlas does the collecting — this is what happens after.

✓ Atlas completion is the trigger. Don't build the folder before it — you'd be guessing at the domain, the service areas and the brand assets, and you'd build the wrong thing twice.
2

Copy the template folder

In 🚀 AM Delivery 2.0, find 🏠 CLIENT TEMPLATE. Right-click it → Duplicate. Rename the copy to the client's name.

Never build a folder by hand. The template carries the 14-day clock, the launch sequence and the safety-net checks. A hand-built folder is missing whichever parts the person building it forgot.

3

What you get, and what to delete

Delete the parents for services this client did not buy. A Foundation client has no SEO card and no Video Production card. Leaving them creates work nobody is doing and a folder nobody trusts.

PackageKeep
Foundation $3,500Google Ads, Meta, Website/CRO, GHL, Creatives, Yelp
Accelerator $5,000Foundation + SEO / AI search
Empire $6,500Accelerator + on-site Video Production

One owner per task. A task with no assignee reaches nobody — it isn't on anyone's list, and it will sit there until launch day.

4

Create the billing card — the step people forget

Without this card, the client never gets billed. Not late — never.

Victor's daily queue is built from 📒 Client Billing Records. A client with no card there does not appear on any day, in any month. They'd get full delivery for free until someone noticed at month close.

Go to 🔒 Accounting → 📒 Client Billing Records, add a task named exactly like the folder, and fill in all four fields:

FieldWhat goes in it
Base Amount$3,500 / $5,000 / $6,500 — or $2,500 / $4,000 / $5,500 in their first two months
Billing DayDay of the month we charge. Ask whoever closed the sale.
Intro EndsThe date they step up to standard — see below
Billing TypeRecurring normally. Upsell for an added service line. Prepaid if they paid upfront.

Intro Ends is the one people get wrong. It's the date the client moves off the first-two-months rate — a $1,000 a month difference.

Set it now, at onboarding, while you know the start date. Nobody can work it out later without digging through payment history, and if it's blank the client quietly stays at the intro rate forever.

Third billing date is the step-up. Start billing 14 October → months 1 and 2 are 14 Oct and 14 Nov → Intro Ends = 14 December.

Upsells get their own card. SEO or video sold alongside the retainer is a separate line on its own billing day — not merged into the main one.

5

Card on file

Chris adds it — into the contact's Payments section in GoHighLevel, and into PayBright. Once it's there it gets charged by clicking, so nobody on the team ever sees or types a number.

Card numbers never go in ClickUp, Slack, email or a spreadsheet. Not once, not temporarily, not to pass along. Last four and expiry are the only card details that belong in a task.
