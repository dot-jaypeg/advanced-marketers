Work it, then say what you did

Click a task. The description tells you what done means — no guessing.

The one rule that matters:

When you finish something, leave a comment saying what you actually did — then mark it Complete.

Closing a task with no note tells the next person nothing. In three weeks nobody remembers which vendor you called or what the quote was.

✕ Don't: mark it Complete and move on.
✓ Do: “Booked Taco Loco for 80 people, $1,400, confirmed for 11:30am drop-off. Invoice in the folder.” → Complete

Already done it? Same thing — drop a comment with what happened, then close it. Don't just close it silently.

Can't finish on time? Move the due date and comment why. That's fine. Silence isn't.

Something broken, confusing, or missing? Post it in 📥 Requests & Improvements — not DMs.
