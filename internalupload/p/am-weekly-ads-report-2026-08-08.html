Lost-revenue risk

  • Zero missed calls this week. All 9 calls were answered, so there is no direct missed-call revenue leak in the Google data.
  • The real leak is downstream of the call, not at pickup. Seven qualified conversations totalling roughly 27 minutes of talk time produced no traceable CRM opportunity and no revenue. Either the calls are not being logged into GoHighLevel, or they are being logged without a Google identifier.
  • Fix the identifier, not the answer rate. Adding a dedicated call-tracking number per Google campaign, or a gclid-stamped call record, would convert this blind spot into attributable pipeline and would very likely change the Google CPL of $418.06.

The most important finding this week

  • Volume and revenue are coming from two different creatives. Video 9 Testimonial (Lincoln) Subbed produced 43 of 63 Meta leads at a $24.30 CPL and closed nothing. Ad 1 | Video 8 (Ish) produced 19 leads at more than double the CPL, $58.80, and closed both sales for $2,999.99.
  • Judging these ads on CPL would lead to the wrong decision. On CPL alone Video 9 looks like the clear winner and Ad 1 looks expensive. On cash, Ad 1 returned 2.69x and Video 9 returned nothing.
  • Recommended test: hold Ad 1 | Video 8 (Ish) budget or increase it, and give Video 9 one more week before judging it. Forty-three leads with zero closes could be a genuine quality problem or simply a sales-cycle lag, and one week of data cannot separate those two.
  • Video 9 Testimonial (Lincoln) | Learn More CTA - Copy 2 is effectively not running at $13.20 and 382 impressions. Either fund it properly or turn it off so it stops fragmenting the ad set.
What cannot be traced to a creative Google call sales cannot be tied to a creative, because the calls run on campaign-level call assets rather than ad-level tracking. This week that is a non-issue since Google produced no attributable sales, but it would matter the moment it does. The Carter Plumbing affiliate sale and the Vanessa Garcia organic sale have no paid creative by definition. The Eric Orduna diagnosis purchase came from an inbound call with no ad identifier, so its true channel is unconfirmed and it is reported under Organic / Other rather than assigned to a paid channel.
