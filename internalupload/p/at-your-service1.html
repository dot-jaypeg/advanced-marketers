Definition · CPQL

Cost Per Qualified Lead, not just any lead.

Most agencies brag about CPL (cost per lead). We track CPQL, the cost per qualified lead, meaning a real person with actual intent to book a service. Junk gets filtered out before it hits your CSR. CPQL = Ad Spend ÷ Qualified Leads. If you're paying $65 per lead but only 50% are qualified, your real CPQL is $130. As lead quality ramps over time, your CPQL drops, even when CPL stays flat.
