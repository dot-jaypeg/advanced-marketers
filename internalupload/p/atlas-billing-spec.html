Atlas Sales Form — Billing Fields

What the sales onboarding form needs to capture so a new client automatically gets a billing record, and nobody has to remember to make one.

Why this matters

Atlas currently holds 147 accounts, 119 of them live. ClickUp holds 91 billing cards. That gap is real money: Signature Plumbing, Capri Plumbing, At Your Service Heating & Cooling and M. L Bernie Co. are all live in Atlas with running ad projects and a linked ClickUp folder — and none of them appear on the billing queue on any day of any month.

The cause is structural, not carelessness. Atlas knows the client. Nothing has ever known the deal. Package, price and billing day live in the contract and in someone's memory.

What Atlas already gets right — don't rebuild these. integrations.clickupFolderId is populated on 122 accounts, so the join to ClickUp already exists. googleMccId (67 live) and metaAdAccountId (52 live) already map clients to ad accounts, which is what the billing run needs to total ad spend.

Fields to add

FieldTypeNotes
package REQenum foundation · accelerator · empire · website_only · custom
baseAmount REQdecimal The agreed monthly, in dollars. Do not derive this from package alone — live rates run from $99 to $7,500 and plenty of clients are on legacy pricing. The form should prefill from package and let the rep override.
billingDay REQint 1–31 Day of month we charge. This is what the daily billing queue sorts on. A client with no billing day never appears on any day.
firstChargeDate REQdate The date of the first charge. Everything below derives from it.
introPricing REQbool Are they on the first-two-months rate? ($1,000 off any tier.)
introEndsDatedate, computed Never hand-entered. See below.
billingType REQenum recurring · upsell · one_time · prepaid · autopay
merchant REQenum stripe_ghl · paybright. Determines where the charge runs and where its revenue is reported.
prepaidMonths optint Required when billingType = prepaid. Base is $0 until it lapses; the 10% of ad spend is still charged every month.
parentAccountId optref Required when billingType = upsell. An upsell is a second billing line on an existing client, on its own billing day — not a duplicate account.
salesRep REQref The field already exists and is null on all 147 accounts. Make it required. When billing has a question, this is who gets asked.
recordType REQenum account · one_off_charge. About eight "accounts" in Atlas are person names — Dan Smith, Ramon Gonzalez, Steven Cervantes — one-off charges filed as clients. Separating them stops them polluting every client count.

Compute Intro Ends — don't ask for it

This single rule is worth about $1,000 per client per month.

Clients on the intro rate step up to standard after two months. Nothing currently forces it. The existing billing sheet has "goes to 3500" typed into 15 different cells as somebody's manual reminder — that is the failure, already written down.

if introPricing:
    introEndsDate = firstChargeDate + 2 months
else:
    introEndsDate = null

First charge 14 Oct → months 1 and 2 are 14 Oct and 14 Nov → Intro Ends = 14 Dec, the third billing date.

Signing is the only moment anyone reliably knows the start date. Ask for it later and nobody can work it out without digging through payment history — so they don't, and the client sits on intro pricing forever.

Writing the ClickUp billing card

On sale close — or on Atlas onboarding completion, either trigger works — create one task in the private Accounting space.

POST  api.clickup.com/api/v2/list/901419708008/task
      📒 Client Billing Records  ·  🔒 Accounting (private)

name = companyName, matching the delivery folder exactly

custom_fields:
  bf9c1a74-198f-4934-99ee-601f9715ce24   Base Amount    currency
  7b93b97c-5611-4d9f-9b0c-9db588f245d5   Billing Day    number 1-31
  0c3e764a-a523-4f89-98bf-b60737929085   Intro Ends     date (ms epoch)
  317714e7-83da-463a-aed6-84220b6c238a   Billing Type   dropdown

Billing Type option ids:
  Recurring              8ca3792e-7b8c-42ab-ba7e-1ac171a66102
  Upsell                 92ccd771-7daf-44f0-ba02-a8fde7ca7cfe
  One-time               cc249fdd-ba01-4ab7-8118-2c02017c0326
  Prepaid                b59f4659-f574-404d-89bb-ae93a69f7dbc
  Autopay - verify only  (PayBright runs it; biller confirms, never charges)

Store the returned task id back on the Atlas account — integrations.clickupBillingTaskId — alongside the folder id. That makes the link two-way and lets a later sync update the card instead of creating a second one.

Fail loudly

Do not create a card with blank fields. If baseAmount or billingDay is missing, refuse the write and raise the error to a human.

A half-filled card is worse than no card. The onboarding checklist gets ticked, the folder looks complete, and the client still never gets charged — but now nobody is looking for the problem. A missing card is at least visibly missing.

Same rule on recordType = one_off_charge: create no recurring billing card at all. It's a single charge, not a client.

One thing to decide before building

Which event creates the card — sale close, or Atlas onboarding completion?

Sale close is earlier and safer: the billing record exists before delivery starts, so a client can never be served before they're billable. But the client may not have completed Atlas yet, so the account record is thinner.

Onboarding completion is cleaner data but later, and it leaves a window where a client is in delivery with no billing record — which is precisely the gap that produced the 51.

Recommendation: sale close. The billing card should exist the moment money is owed, not the moment paperwork is finished.
