How Screening Works

Brianna · Start at the top and work down. When in doubt, ask.
1
Every morning

Check Indeed

Open new applications. Read each resume against the two rules in the black box below.

2

Do they pass the two rules?

Yes → go

Message them on Indeed with the booking link. Message #1.

No → stop

Send the decline message. Message #5. Takes 20 seconds. Always send it.

3
15 minutes

Run the call

Open the script. Make your copy. Read it top to bottom and type notes as you go. Do not wing it.

Never promise them an interview. Tell them you are doing more calls and they will get an email if it is a fit.

4
Within 5 minutes of hanging up

Score them out of 25

Five things, one to five each. Use the table at the bottom. Score it while it is fresh.

5

What the score tells you to do

20 – 25  Send it

Email them the assessment. Message #2. Same day.

15 – 19  Ask

Post your notes in Slack. Ask the manager if you should send the assessment.

Under 15  Pass

Send the decline. Message #5.

6
They get 48 hours

Wait for the assessment

Remind them once at 24 hours. That is the only chase you do.

Came back in time

Post it in Slack for the manager, same day.

Missed 48 hours

They are out. Send the decline. Do not extend it.

7
Manager has 24 hours

Manager reviews the assessment

Approved, or no reply

Send the booking link. Message #3. No reply in 24 hours means send it anyway.

Manager says no

Send the decline. Message #5.

8
Day before the Zoom

Send the reminder

Message #4. Then you are done. The manager takes it from here.

The Two Rules

Move them forward

  • 4+ years doing this exact job
  • Stayed 2+ years somewhere at least once

Stop right there

  • Under 3 years experience
  • New job every single year
  • Trashed an old boss on the call
  • Could not give you a real number

Score sheet · 1 to 5 each · 25 total

1Experience — enough years, right kind of work
2Job history — did they stay put anywhere
3Real answers — gave you actual numbers, fast
4Talking — clear and easy to follow
5Wants it — asked questions, knows who we are
Not sure about someone? Score them 15–19 and ask. Nobody gets in trouble for asking.
