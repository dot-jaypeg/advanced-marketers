Ish — Batch 2: Shorts (20–30 sec)

One pain point per video. Hit the cold open in the first 3 seconds — no wind-up, no intro — then the bullets, close fast. Talk, don't read. Drop a "let me tell you something" on the biggest claim.

QUALIFY WITHOUT SOUNDING LIKE EVERY OTHER AD
Skip the "if you're a contractor doing over $2M..." opener — everybody runs that. Instead, qualify inside the story: say "your trucks," "your techs," "your install base," "you're running a $2, $3 mil shop." Owners feel called out; everybody else keeps scrolling. Same filter, none of the formula.

SHORT 7 — NEXGEN STORY (can run 45–60s)

Ads didn't build NexGen. Retention did.

Cold open"Look guys — everybody thinks I grew NexGen to $100 million with ads. Let me tell you the truth."
  • Yes, we spent on marketing. But ads only ever did ONE job: get us in the door the first time.
  • The real machine was retention. Tight follow-up, maintenance plans, staying in that home — every customer we won, we KEPT. That's what compounds to $100M.
  • If I had run ads only? Let me tell you what happens — I'd have been buying the same customers back every 5 years, bleeding $300–500 a lead, and my competitors would've been living in homes I paid to win. That business never breaks $10M.
  • Back then I had to build retention by hand — people, phone calls, systems. Nuve is that machine in a box: your logo on the wall, notifications when their system needs you, the customer locked in for life.
  • Story close: "Ads get you the customer once. Retention makes you rich. I lived it — now I built it for you."
SHORT 8 — NEXGEN STORY

The customers we lost at NexGen

Cold open"At NexGen, I watched customers we PAID to win call somebody else. Let me tell you what that taught me."
  • We spent big on marketing. Won the job, did great work, left the house — and owned nothing in that home.
  • Two years later their system acts up, they Google, and some other company's truck is in THEIR driveway. A customer I paid for.
  • That's when it clicked: the job isn't won when you close the sale. It's won when you're still in the home five years later.
  • That's the problem Nuve was built to kill — your brand stays on the wall, so the next call is yours.
