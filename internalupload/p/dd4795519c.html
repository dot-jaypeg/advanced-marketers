Found In The Recordings

Two fixable reasons callers give up
Omar's callback number displays as "SCAM" on caller ID. Mike Hart said it to his face on the Apr 14 call: "your number shows as a 626 scam." Customers are screening out BluePoint's own callbacks. Fix: register the outbound numbers with the carriers' branded-caller-ID services (Free Caller Registry / First Orion / Hiya).
The voicemail box was FULL at least twice (Mar 17 and Mar 27) — callers heard "mailbox is full" and physically couldn't leave a message. Those callers are gone with no trace.
And the CRM has never been worked: 100 opportunities all parked in "New Lead," $0 values, zero marked won or lost — which is why 6 of the 7 qualified deals above have no recorded quote or outcome. The pipeline stages exist; nobody moves the cards.

The Plan

In priority order
Call the six named missed buyers this week — Joe Ramirez (the GC) first. Scripts write themselves: "You reached out about your pool project and we want to make it right."
Close the loop on the seven qualified deals. What happened at Mark's, Anthony's, Cynthia's, Kimi's estimates? Did Mary's Jun 26 visit happen? Get David Vega's design meeting booked. Put every answer in the CRM with a dollar value.
Fix caller ID ("SCAM") and the voicemail box. Zero-cost fixes that recover callbacks the ads already paid for.
Answer rate & tracked lines: only ~15% of callers reach a human. Live coverage during ad hours plus every ad number routed through tracked lines — every point of answer rate is worth ~$14k of pipeline at this account's numbers.
