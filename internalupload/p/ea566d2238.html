Give Your AC the Attention It Deserves

Chicago summers bring humidity, afternoon storms, and long AC run times. Most comfort complaints are airflow and moisture problems long before they’re “system failures.” Here are a few things that can help your central air last through August:

  • 1.

    Let your system run steady to control humidity.
    Big thermostat swings make Wilmette homes feel clammy and can worsen comfort. This is especially true for heat pumps, and ductless systems.

  • 2.

    Keep the outdoor unit clear and gently rinse the coil.
    Cottonwood and grime act like a blanket. The resulting blockage makes the system act like it’s low on refrigerant.

  • 3.

    Hot bedrooms are usually a ductwork issue.
    Closed doors, undersized returns, and leaky duct runs are common in older homes in Skokie. We can correct them.

  • 4.

    If you have a heat pump, make sure the thermostat is configured correctly.
    You don’t want to call out an HVAC company because you forgot to switch things to cool.
