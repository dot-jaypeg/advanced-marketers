Show-Rate Defense & No-Show Playbook

+

Brandon already runs a strong no-show recovery — manually. We systematize it so it survives ad volume.

Before the demo (automate)

  • Instant confirmation email + SMS at booking — with Brandon's/AE's direct number ("things change fast in HVAC — text me, we'll reschedule, no problem").
  • Reminders at 24h and 1h, short pre-demo video from Ish so they know who they're meeting and why it's worth showing.
  • Calendar invite verified delivered — check the spam-folder problem; it happened on a live call this week.
  • Never book past 72 hours out. Booked-a-week-out demos with gatekeepers are where shows go to die.

After a no-show (systematize Brandon's play)

  • Multi-text + multi-call from the SDR and Brandon personally — two voices, more pressure.
  • Still ghosting after days → group chat with Ish for credibility leverage. It works; make it a defined stage in HubSpot, not a judgment call.
  • Sequences templated in HubSpot so recovery runs the same at 10 leads/day as it does at 2.
