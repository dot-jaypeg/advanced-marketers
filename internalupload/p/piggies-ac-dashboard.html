Why the misses matter beyond the lost lead: Google's smart bidding learns from call outcomes. Long, connected calls (like the 17-minute Jun 17 call and the 17-minute Jul 1 tune-up call) register as conversions — talk time is a buying signal — so Google finds more searchers like those callers. Missed and seconds-long calls register as non-conversions, teaching the algorithm those searchers are the wrong audience and quietly steering budget away from exactly the buyers it found. The misses cluster around 8–9 AM: extending live coverage to the first hour of the morning closes most of the gap.
