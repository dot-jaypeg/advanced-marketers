Reminders for everyone

Same three every week until they stop appearing
1. Never take a password on a recorded line. Not spoken, not typed into chat, not “just send it to me.” The client resets it themselves, or adds us as a user. MFA codes count too. Cards are fine — passwords are not.
2. Pre-pulled numbers are the spine of the weekly call. Spend, CPL, cost per booked job, in hand before you join. “How have the leads been?” is backwards — we track, we tell.
3. Close every call out loud: owner, date, channel, next meeting. Not one call this week ran the full recap segment. “I'll send you an update” is not a commitment.
Every client call is QA-reviewed and every go-live is tracked against the day-14 SLA. Client messaging is always week 2 — never week 4, never “about a month.” If something is blocking a launch, escalate it by day 10; a blocker you raise is never held against you, a blocker you sit on is.
