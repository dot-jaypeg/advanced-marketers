01 · The Findings

Eight structural problems in the account.

Every finding below is documented in reports pulled directly from account 530-138-9572 on August 17, 2026, or read off the live pages the same day. Window is 90 days, May 19 to August 16, 2026. Not opinion. Data.

Finding 01 · Critical

The largest conversion action in the account is a tag Google has flagged as broken.

"Phone Call" fired 187.50 times in the window, more than any other action. Its source reads "Website (Import from clicks)", its status reads Needs attention, it is set to Primary, and it is included in account level goals. Google is telling you the tag is unreliable and bidding against it at the same time. Every budget decision for the last year has been shaped by it.

Verify it yourself Goals → Conversions → Summary → View all conversion actions.
It sits under the group "Other", carrying the Needs attention flag.
Finding 02 · Warning

42.5% of search spend goes to terms Google will not disclose.

$7,606.84 of the last 30 days went to search terms that never appear in any report, converting at $157.94 against $98.64 for the terms you can see. A 60% premium on nearly half the budget. Every campaign runs exact match with no negative keyword list on the account at all. The complete list of 120 negatives is in section 06.

Verify it yourself Campaigns → Insights and reports → Search terms, last 30 days.
Then Tools → Shared library → Negative keyword lists.
Finding 03 · Critical

94% of form conversions fire on the landing page, before anyone submits anything.

"Submit Lead Form" is a manual event, not a tracked submission. Google reports exactly where it fires. In the last 30 days: 27 on the air duct page, 15 on dryer vent, 3 on insulation, 1 unknown, and 2 on the thank you page. That is 45 of 48 firing on the page the customer is still standing on. A conversion recorded on the landing page is a click on a button. It cannot know whether the form validated, whether it sent, or whether a human is on the other end.

Verify it yourself Goals → Conversions → "Submit Lead Form" → Webpages tab.
The breakdown by URL is on that screen.
Finding 04 · Critical

A call becomes a lead after 10 seconds.

The threshold on "Calls from ads" is set to 10 seconds. Across the 90 days that admitted 33 calls that ran under a minute, 7 of them under 10 seconds, and 2 that were never answered at all. Every one counted as a lead. Smart Bidding has been trained on that mix for 12 months and it has been getting steadily better at finding more of it.

Verify it yourself Goals → Conversions → "Calls from ads" → Edit settings.
The field reads "Count calls longer than 10 seconds".
Finding 05 · Critical

No booked job has ever been reported back to Google.

Zero offline conversion import exists. A form is valued at $10 and a call at $5, against real tickets of roughly $2,000 on air duct, $10,000 on insulation and $15,767 on system replacement. A phone tap and a whole house install currently carry almost the same weight in every bidding decision the account makes.

Verify it yourself Goals → Conversions → Uploads.
The table is empty. No offline import has ever run.
Finding 06 · Critical

One generic form serves all three services, shared across every page.

The form on the page works for the customer. It is also the identical third party iframe on all three landing pages, same URL, same hash, served from st.sendajob.com. Air duct, dryer vent and insulation all submit the same generic form, so nothing records which service or which page produced the lead. On submit the page does not change: no thank you screen, no redirect, no URL change. That means no page load for the Google tag to fire on, and no confirmation for the customer. $48,997.61 of spend ran through those three pages.

Verify it yourself skycleanair.com/air-duct-cleaning-ub-2/
skycleanair.com/dryer-vent-cleaning-ub/
skycleanair.com/insulation-san-diego/
View Page Source, search <form. Zero results on all three.
Finding 07 · Warning

Insulation costs 3.4 times the account average per conversion.

$11,493.96 across the 90 days for 29 conversions, at $396.34 against an account average of $117.11. Ad strength is rated Poor and the campaign is flagged Limited by budget. It bought 13,026 impressions in the quarter, more than dryer vent's 8,267, and returned a quarter of the conversions. Insulation is the best margin service in the business and the worst performing line in the account.

Verify it yourself Campaigns → "Insulation | TCPA Experiment 2".
Custom range May 19 to August 16, 2026.
Finding 08 · Critical

The thank you page exists. The form has never sent anyone to it.

There is a working thank you page at /thank-you-google that reads "Thank You! We've Received Your Information". Google lists it as a conversion page and it is marked No recent conversions. It has recorded 2 in total. It is the one page on the site that could prove a lead, and the form does not route to it. It also carries the wrong phone number, so a customer who somehow reaches it calls an untracked line.

04 · The Fix

Ten things in the first 30 days.

Before a single new campaign launches, the existing account gets fixed. No live campaign is paused at any point.

01

Conversion action teardown, 18 down to 4

Rebuild the Phone Call tag Google has flagged, move Submit Lead Form off the manual click event and onto the thank you page load, archive the duplicate form action and the eight idle Smart Campaign actions, and trace the 254 conversions coming from tags that are not in the enabled list. Rebuild around Qualified Call, Form Lead, Booked Job and Sold Job.

Day 1 to 2
02

Call threshold from 10 seconds to 60

Removes the 33 short events and 2 unanswered calls from the bidding signal immediately. The three minute bar is reported separately from day one so booking conversations become visible as their own number.

Day 1 to 2
03

Tracking numbers across every channel

The site runs two numbers today, 858-346-5551 on the homepage and 858-758-4269 on all three landing pages, plus a stray out of state number sitting in the air duct markup. The thank you page carries the homepage number, so a customer who does reach it calls an untracked line. The paid line gets extended into a full set: paid search, Google Guarantee, Meta, organic, Yelp and each landing page, with recording and transcript scoring on all of them.

Day 2 to 4
04

120 negatives deployed across all campaigns

Eight buckets, applied at campaign level with correct match types, listed in full in section 06. The account currently has none. This is what stops the leak rather than reacting to it after the money is gone.

Day 3 to 5
05

Every channel measured together, not one at a time

Once the leak is stopped, every source gets a tracked entry point: paid search, Google Guarantee, Meta, Yelp, organic, direct and the Business Profile. Offline conversions come back from the CRM tagged by source, so the question stops being "how did Google do" and becomes "where did the booked jobs actually come from". No single platform can answer that, and no single platform should be trusted to grade its own work. This is the report that tells the real story.

Day 5 to 14
06

Real forms, routed to the thank you page, pushed to the CRM

Native forms with service context on each page, replacing the shared iframe. On submit the customer is routed to /thank-you-google, which already exists. That page load becomes the conversion event, which is the only form tracking method that is actually reliable. The same submission fires to the CRM through the API with the source, campaign and service attached, so the lead arrives complete instead of arriving as an email.

Day 3 to 10
07

Heading structure rebuilt

One true H1 per page carrying the service and the city. Air duct carries 9 today, dryer vent 8, insulation 6. The homepage already has exactly one and is the model. Titles, meta descriptions and internal linking realigned behind it.

Day 5 to 10
08

FAQ pages built for answer engines

Structured and marked up for how questions are actually asked, so the business can be surfaced as a direct answer. Written and reviewed by people. Local SEO groundwork runs in parallel.

Day 7 to 14
09

One blended reporting view

Search and Google Guarantee on a single line for the first time, with cost per verified lead by service, updated daily. Budget can finally move to whichever channel is actually producing.

Day 10 to 21
10

Baseline published. Zero new campaigns until it is.

By day 30 there is a defensible cost per verified lead for the first time. Native forms, offline conversion import and ROAS bidding follow in months 2 and 3. Nothing new launches until the existing account is clean and measured.
