The rules everyone works to 05

Announced 11 September 2026. These are not suggestions and they apply to every department.

  1. Everything lives in ClickUp. If there is no task, it never happened.
  2. We communicate in ClickUp. Decisions, approvals, cancels, emails, calls, questions, all of it goes on the task. Slack is for urgent pings only, as in "I sent you something, please look."
  3. Every account has one name on it. Who owns it, what services, what package.
  4. Managers clear the queue and keep it clear.
  5. Morning meetings daily, no later than 9:30 AM PT.
One owner, one due date

A task with two owners has no owner. A task with no due date does not exist as far as the system is concerned. If you are handed work with neither, that is the first thing to fix, not the last.

For whoever is training someone 06

If you are onboarding a new person, this page is the syllabus. Send them this link, not a folder, not a zip, not a doc you exported.

How to run it

  • Send this page first. Everything else is reachable from it. They only ever need one bookmark.
  • Go in order of what they will touch. A growth hire reads Cancels & Saves before anything else. Someone joining onboarding starts at Section 03.
  • Have them do it live, not read it. Open the ClickUp list next to the guide and file a real card.
  • If a guide is wrong, tell Christian. Do not write a corrected version of your own. The page gets fixed and everybody gets the fix at once.
Do not make a copy of anything on this page

No duplicated docs, no personal versions, no "final v2" in a Drive folder. The moment there are two copies of a process, half the team is following the wrong one and nobody finds out until a client is angry.

If you need something that is not here, ask for it to be added here.

What is not on this page 07

Weekly ads reports, revenue trackers, QA dashboards, audit snapshots and client landing pages also live on this domain, but they are point-in-time documents, not process. They go stale by design and they are not linked here on purpose.

If you are looking for how we do something, it is on this page. If you are looking for what happened in a particular week, ask for that report directly.
