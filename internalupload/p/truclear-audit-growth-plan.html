02 · What's Actually Broken

Six specific problems bleeding your revenue.

Nothing about the product or the sales team. The economics work. What's broken is the machinery around the machine. Every one of these is fixable, and most of them fix inside 30-60 days.

Problem 01 · Speed-to-Lead

Meta leads sit for hours before anyone calls.

Current process: Meta lead comes in → Gabriel gets it on Instagram → sends a text → if no response, calls "later in the day." That's your entire pipeline waiting minutes-to-hours between fill and first human contact.

Industry data: leads called under 5 minutes are 21× more likely to qualify than leads called at 30 minutes. 78% of homeowners hire the FIRST company that responds.
Problem 02 · Meta Account Reset

Chamber Media rebuilt from scratch and killed your pixel history.

You had a Meta pixel that had been learning since April, feeding on the $90-100K revenue Gabriel was generating in the first two months. Then a new agency spun up a new Meta ad account, which means: new pixel, new learning phase, zero historical conversion data. Meta was starting over with no memory of what a qualified lead looks like for TruClear.

The old account still exists. The pixel data still exists. Reactivating that account (or migrating audiences) is a same-day fix.
Problem 03 · Wrong Optimization Target

Chamber is optimizing for "cost per lead," not qualified leads or revenue.

Result: 50 Meta leads in a week, only 2 converted. Meta is delivering exactly what it was told to deliver — cheap leads — because nothing is telling it which leads are actually valuable. There's no offline conversion tracking feeding qualified/not-qualified data back to Meta, so the algorithm can't learn who your real buyer looks like.

Erik said it clearly on the call: "I don't care if the cost per lead is $100 or $120. It doesn't matter to us. It's getting the booking done."
Problem 04 · Weak Lead Form

Your lead form is too easy to fill out. Tire-kickers welcome.

A basic name/email/phone form filters out nobody. Meta will find you anyone who's mildly curious about clean water, then send them straight to your sales team. The result is what you're seeing: high volume, sub-5% close rate, and burned-out sales reps.

Fix in Section 05: add 5-7 pre-qualifying questions to filter out renters, tire-kickers, and unqualified budgets before your sales team ever sees the lead.
Problem 05 · Creative Doesn't Sell

AI-driven b-roll videos with zero real people. A kid at a faucet as your best static.

Water filtration is a trust sale. Homeowners spend $5,000 when they see another homeowner say "this changed my house." Right now every video is generic b-roll — house exteriors, landscapes, no faces, no stories. The one static ad shows a kid drinking water, which is charming but tells nobody why they should call. These are remarketing/brand-awareness videos being used to convert cold prospecting traffic. Wrong tool for the job.

Fix: real customers face-to-camera reviewing their experience · homeowner-narrated install walkthroughs · before/after water test demos · housewife-persona storytelling. On-site video shoots twice per month starting Week 1.
Problem 06 · Locations Underutilized

You have 5 locations. Only Irvine is generating anything.

Two physical (Anaheim + Irvine), Brea coming soon, plus 3 organic Google Business Profile locations. Only Irvine shows up in search. The other 4 locations are dark. That's 80% of your local search real estate sitting empty.

What's Wrong With The Creative

These ads don't sell.

  • AI-generated feel across all 4 ads — generic, soulless, could be for any brand. No specificity to TruClear, Orange County, or actual water problems.
  • Zero real people on camera in any of the videos — just b-roll of house exteriors, landscapes, and stock-looking scenes. Homeowners don't buy $5K systems from b-roll.
  • The static ad shows a kid at a faucet — a kid can't tell you what changed after the install, what the water tastes like now, or how much scale disappeared. You need actual customers on camera reviewing their real experience.
  • These feel like remarketing / brand-awareness content, not conversion-focused sales videos. Brand videos have a job (warming a familiar audience), but you're using them to convert cold Meta traffic — wrong tool for the job.
  • No emotional payoff, no transformation story — no "here's what my water looked like before / here's what changed / here's how my family feels now."
  • 4 ads · all launched same day (Aug 28) · all pushing the same "$500 off summer event" — one offer, no persona variance, no rolling test methodology. Ad #4 already at <100 impressions and Meta has judged it.
  • Even with the best pre-qualification form in the world, these ads won't sell — the video itself has to do the emotional work of moving a stranger from "eh, water's fine" to "I need this in my house."
What Sells Water Filtration

Real people. Real transformation.

  • Real customer testimonials, face-to-camera — mom/dad in their actual kitchen describing what changed. "My skin used to feel dry every shower. Two weeks in, I noticed." That's what sells $5K systems.
  • Homeowner-narrated install walkthroughs — the owner walking through their own house showing the tech installing, drinking the water at the end, tasting the difference on camera.
  • Before/after water test demos — real water sample from their tap, real TDS meter reading, real strip test showing chlorine/hardness levels. Visual proof they can't argue with.
  • Housewife-persona videos ("I was so worried about what my kids were drinking...") targeting the exact ideal customer profile Gabriel called out on the call.
  • Technician POV / trust-building content — meet-the-tech style videos showing expertise. "Here's what I look for when I test water in an Anaheim home."
  • Video structure that sells: Hook (3 sec) → Problem (name their pain) → Agitate (why it matters for their family) → Proof (real customer/test result) → Offer (free in-home test) → CTA (book now).
  • Save the b-roll/brand feel for warm retargeting audiences — for cold Meta prospecting, you need people, problems, and proof, not aesthetic shots of houses.

We'll validate the exact setup once we're in the ad account, and either way the pre-qualification questions in Section 05 apply.

The bottom line:

Chamber is running 4 AI-feeling b-roll videos with no humans and one static of a kid — for a product that ONLY sells when a homeowner sees another homeowner say "this changed my house." Fix the creative (real people, real testimonials, real installations) and add the pre-qualification questions from Section 05, and you're looking at 4-5× lift on the same ad spend.

The 5 questions to add.

Ordered by qualifying weight. Each question does double duty: filters out bad leads AND gives your sales rep ammunition to close on the first call.

  • Do you own or rent your home?
    Own QualifiedRent Disqualify — route to renter offer or nurture
    Why: Whole-home water filtration requires permanent installation. Renters can't buy your primary product. Filtering this ONE question alone eliminates 15-30% of unqualified traffic.
  • What's the main water problem you want to solve? (Select all that apply)
    Bad-tasting drinking water · Hard water / mineral buildup · Chlorine smell/taste · Skin or hair issues · Health concerns about contaminants · Recent water test showed problems · Just want cleaner water for family
    Why: Answer reveals problem intensity + sales angle. "Recent water test showed problems" = HOT LEAD. Sales rep opens the call already knowing which system to pitch and which pain point to press.
  • When are you looking to have a system installed?
    This month Hot — priority routingNext 2-3 months WarmJust researching for now Nurture sequence
    Why: Timeline is the single strongest buying signal. "This month" gets called in 60 seconds. "Just researching" gets a 30-day email nurture sequence instead of a call — protecting your sales team's time.
  • What's your budget range for a whole-home system?
    Under $2,000 Below product range — route to nurture or entry offer$2,000 - $5,000 Standard fit$5,000 - $10,000 Premium fitNot sure — want to see options Consultation route
    Why: Your ticket is $5K. If someone budgets under $2K, they're not your customer today. This question also lets your rep drop straight into product tiers without wasting 20 minutes on discovery.
  • How many people live in your home?
    1-2 · 3-4 · 5+
    Why: Determines system size + capacity + pricing tier before the call. Also a soft health/family angle — more people = more urgency about water quality. Sales rep opens with the exact system fit, not a discovery process.

Within 30 days Meta has enough data to stop optimizing for cheap leads and start optimizing for buyers who look like your buyers.
