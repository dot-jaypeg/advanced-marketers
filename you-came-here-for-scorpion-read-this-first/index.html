The Big Agency Trap

Your current agency is
profiting from your confusion.

When private equity buys a marketing agency, contractors commonly report the same pattern. Tools, contracts, and proprietary systems make leaving costlier than staying. The three problems below come up across publicly available reviews on Glassdoor, Clutch, BBB, and industry publications.

PROBLEM 01 // // THE LOCK
01

You do not own your website.

Their websites run on a proprietary CMS. When you leave, the site stays with them. Your SEO equity, your conversion rate history, your custom integrations, all gone. You start from zero in a space where compounding work matters most.

PROBLEM 02 // // THE TRAP
02

12 to 24 month contract trap.

Long term contracts with reported buyout fees in the tens of thousands of dollars to exit early. You signed up for marketing services. Many contractors report ending up trapped, paying for a system they can no longer escape.

PROBLEM 03 // // THE NOISE
03

High volume, low quality leads.

The dashboard looks great. The booked jobs do not match. Account managers running 30 plus accounts cannot tell the difference between a real lead and a tire kicker, so the metric they show you is volume.
